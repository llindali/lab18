# COST — Buffett-inspired business memo

**Verdict: OWN THE BUSINESS (quality assessment only; price not evaluated).**

Costco shows the sort of consistently productive business economics a patient owner appreciates. ROE was 28.3% at the FY2022 year-end and 25.8% at the FY2026 year-end, and it exceeded the 15% classroom hurdle in every fiscal year. Owner earnings, defined for this exercise as operating cash flow less capital spending, rose from $3,501 million in FY2022 to $9,382 million in FY2026. Net income rose from $5,844 million to $9,226 million over the same period. All three prescribed checks pass.

These numbers support durable earning power, but financial statements alone do not prove the competitive moat, management quality, or price attractiveness. Full owner earnings would require a nuanced view of maintenance versus growth capital spending. This is a judgment about the business, not a recommendation to buy the stock at any particular price.

**Do I trust this verdict?** I trust the direction of the business-quality assessment because the reported five-year profitability and cash generation support it; I would still need qualitative moat and management research before treating it as a complete investment decision.
